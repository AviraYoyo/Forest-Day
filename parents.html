How to help at home 家長在家可以這樣支援

Picture books we recommend 延伸閱讀書單

Nine real picture books that connect to Forest Day. One is used in the lesson itself; the rest are for the classroom corner or reading at home after the trip. 九本現成繪本。只有《We're Going on a Bear Hunt》在課堂上誦唱（請用實體書），其餘放圖書角或活動後親子共讀。最後兩本由本地出版，有香港在地連結。

Where to find them 去哪裡找
Search the Hong Kong Public Library catalogue (webcat.hkpl.gov.hk) by title or ISBN, or buy from a local bookshop (Bookazine, Dymocks, 商務, 三聯, 誠品). 用書名或 ISBN 在香港公共圖書館目錄搜尋並預約，或到書店購買。

A note on rules 關於規則的重要立場
We never say "You must not climb." Hong Kong Forest Adventures' philosophy is that children take risks, and that is how they grow. Our must not rules only cover things that can hurt the child or others (running on wet rocks, pushing, going alone). Everything else becomes "You must go slowly. You must look. You must try." 本單元絕不會教「你不可以爬」。HKFA 的理念是孩子需要冒險才會成長。我們的 must not 只針對會傷己傷人的事，其餘一律轉為「你要慢慢走、先看一看、要試試看」。
