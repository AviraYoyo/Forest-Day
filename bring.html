🎒 What to Bring

Check your bag with a grown-up. Tick the box when it is in your bag!
All-weather adventure! 🌧️☀️
We play outside in every weather. If it rains, we wear our raincoats and play in the puddles — and that is the best part! 這是全天氣活動：落雨照去，穿好雨衣踩水花才是最好玩的部分。請勿因天氣不佳而缺席。

You must… ✔

  • stay with your group 跟著組別
  • wear water shoes in the river 在溪邊穿水鞋
  • go slowly on the rocks 在石頭上慢慢走
  • try something new 嘗試新事物
  • listen to the teachers 聽老師的話

You must not… ✘

  • run on the rocks 在石頭上跑
  • push your friends 推朋友
  • go alone 獨自走
  • forget your hat 忘記戴帽子

How to get there 🗺️ 交通

Tai Shui Hang Campus: 12D Tai Shui Hang North Road, Tai Shui Hang Village. About 5 minutes' walk from Tai Shui Hang MTR Station, Exit B. 地址：大水坑北村路 12D 號。港鐵大水坑站 B 出口步行約 5 分鐘。
