All-weather adventure! 🌧️☀️
We play outside in every weather. If it rains, we wear our raincoats and play in the puddles — and that is the best part!

You must… ✔

  • stay with your group
  • wear slippers in the river
  • go slowly on the rocks
  • try something new
  • listen to the teachers

You must not… ✘

  • run on the rocks
  • push your friends
  • go alone
  • forget your hat

How to get there 🗺️

Tai Shui Hang Campus: 12D Tai Shui Hang North Road, Tai Shui Hang Village. About 5 minutes' walk from Tai Shui Hang MTR Station, Exit B.
